# LabelSure

### AI-Powered Legal Metrology Compliance Ecosystem

**From Package Image to Explainable Compliance Decision.**

LabelSure helps **Legal Metrology Inspectors, manufacturers, and consumers** analyze product labels and identify potential compliance issues using OCR, AI-assisted extraction, and an evidence-backed legal compliance engine.

## 🚀 How It Works

```text
Label Image
    ↓
OCR & Information Extraction
    ↓
Legal Metrology Checks
    ↓
Evidence & Explanation
    ↓
Compliance Result
```

## 👥 Three Workspaces

**🏛️ ENFORCE** — For inspectors to scan products, review evidence, manage inspections, and generate reports.

**🏭 PREVENT** — For manufacturers to check packaging, identify issues, and re-scan before products reach the market.

**🛒 VERIFY** — For consumers to understand product information and report potential issues.

Each workspace has **role-based access** and users must log out before entering another stakeholder workspace.

## ⚖️ Legal Knowledge Base

The compliance engine uses the **Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (Act No. 1 of 2010)** as its legal source.

Results are classified as:

* 🟢 **Act-Verified**
* 🔵 **Rule-Dependent**
* 🟠 **Requires Verification**
* 🔴 **Potential Non-Compliance**

The system does not treat uncertain OCR results as legal violations or invent missing regulations.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
* **Backend:** Supabase, PostgreSQL
* **Authentication:** Supabase Auth
* **Storage:** Supabase Storage
* **Processing:** OCR + AI-assisted information extraction
* **Compliance:** Deterministic legal rule engine

## 📌 Project Status

LabelSure is currently a **functional prototype** demonstrating label scanning, OCR, legal compliance checks, evidence-backed findings, role-based workspaces, inspections, manufacturer checks, and consumer reporting.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

LabelSure provides **AI-assisted compliance screening**, not legal certification. Final legal determination remains with the competent authority.
